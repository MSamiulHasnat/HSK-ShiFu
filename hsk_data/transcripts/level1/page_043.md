## 课文 Text

### 1 在学校 **In the school** 🎧 05-1

Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén?
A: 你家有几口人？

Wǒ jiā yǒu sān kǒu rén.
B: 我家有三口人。

[photo: a man and two children (a boy and a girl) posing together smiling]

**English Version**
A: How many people are there in your family?
B: There are three.

**New Words**
1. 家 jiā n. family
2. 有 yǒu v. to have, there be
*3. 口 kǒu m. a measure word for members of families, etc.
