### 2 认识独体字 Single-Component Characters

（1）"月"，表示月亮。
"月" refers to the moon.

[diagram: pictographic evolution of 月: a crescent moon shape → a crescent with an internal line → a more angular version → 月]
yuè
[handwriting practice grid showing character 月 traced in 4 strokes, in green ink:丿 冂 月 月]

（2）"心"，表示心脏。
"心" refers to the heart.

[diagram: pictographic evolution of 心: a heart-organ pictograph → a stylized version → a more angular version → 心]
xīn
[handwriting practice grid showing character 心 traced in 4 strokes, in green ink: 丶 心 心 心]

（3）"中"，本义是飘扬的旗子，现在表示方位，意思是"中间"。
The basic meaning of "中" is "flying flag". It is now a word of locality, meaning "middle".

[diagram: pictographic evolution of 中: a pole with flags/streamers through a circle → a stylized version → a more angular version → 中]
zhōng
[handwriting practice grid showing character 中 traced in 4 strokes, in green ink: 丨 ㄇ 口 中]

（4）"人"，表示直立的人。
"人" originally looked like a person standing straight.

[diagram: pictographic evolution of 人: a bent-leg standing figure pictograph → a simplified version → a more angular version → 人]
rén
[handwriting practice grid showing character 人 traced in 2 strokes, in green ink: 丿 人]
