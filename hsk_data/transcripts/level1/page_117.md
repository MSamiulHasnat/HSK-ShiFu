## 课文 Text

### 1 在宿舍 In the dorm 🔊 14-1

Zuótiān shàngwǔ nǐ qù nǎr le?
A: 昨天 上午 你 去 哪儿 了？

Wǒ qù shāngdiàn mǎi dōngxi le.
B: 我 去 商店 买 东西 了。

Nǐ mǎi shénme le?
A: 你 买 什么 了？

Wǒ mǎile yìdiǎnr píngguǒ.
B: 我 买了 一点儿 苹果。

[photo: a woman in a grocery store selecting produce, holding what appears to be a piece of fruit]

**English Version**
A: Where did you go yesterday morning?
B: I went shopping.
A: What did you buy?
B: I bought some apples.

**New Words**
1. 东西　dōngxi　n.　thing, stuff
2. 一点儿　yìdiǎnr　num.-m.　a few, a little
3. 苹果　píngguǒ　n.　apple
