(2) "女"，字形像一个跪在地上的女人，意思是"女人"。
"女" originally looked like a woman kneeling down on the ground, meaning "woman".

(3) "了"，字形像子（头朝下、未出生的胎儿）的倒写，头朝上，表示已经出生的婴儿。现在成为虚词。
"了" originally looked like a reversed 子 (an unborn foetus in a head-down position). With the head on top, it referred to a baby already born. Now it is a function word.

[illustration: stroke evolution diagram (reversed pictograph) → (pictograph) → 了, labeled le, with character grid showing stroke order 了 了]

(4) "大"，本义是张开双手双腿顶天立地的人，现在意思与"小"相对。
"大" originally referred to a person in a standing position, with his arms and legs stretching out. Now it means "big", opposite to "小 (small)".
