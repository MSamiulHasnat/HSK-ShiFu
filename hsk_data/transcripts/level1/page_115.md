## 汉字 Characters

### 1 认识独体字 Single-Component Characters

(1) "日"是太阳的形象，本义是太阳。
The character "日" uses the image of the sun and its basic meaning is "the sun".

[illustration: character evolution diagram showing a circle with a dot progressing through intermediate forms into the character 日, labeled "rì"]
[illustration: stroke-order practice grid showing 日 written across boxes: 丨, 冂, 日, 日]

(2) "目"是眼睛的形象，本义是眼睛。
The character "目" uses the image of an eye and its basic meaning is "eye".

[illustration: character evolution diagram showing an eye shape progressing through intermediate forms into the character 目, labeled "mù"]
[illustration: stroke-order practice grid showing 目 written across boxes: 丨, 冂, 月, 月, 目]

(3) "习"，本义是"学"过后再反复地温习，达到熟练的程度，现在"学"和"习"没什么差异。
The original meaning of "习" was to learn and repeatedly review to achieve proficiency. In modern Chinese, however, it means pretty much the same as "学 (to study/learn)".

[illustration: character evolution diagram showing wing-like ancient forms progressing through intermediate forms into the character 习, labeled "xí"]
[illustration: stroke-order practice grid showing 习 written across boxes: 丁, 刁, 习]
