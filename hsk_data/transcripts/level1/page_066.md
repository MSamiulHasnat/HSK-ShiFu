**1　认识独体字　Single-Component Characters**

（1）"四"，表示数量"4"。
"四" means "four".

[character evolution diagram: 三 → 乢 → 四(round) → 四]　sì
[stroke order grid: ノ 冂 冂 四 四]

（2）"五"，表示数量"5"。
"五" means "five".

[character evolution diagram: 乂(hourglass shape) → 乂 → 乂 → 五]　wǔ
[stroke order grid: 一 ナ 开 五]

（3）"书"，本义是将毛笔放在墨池中蘸墨以便涂写，现在是"书写、书籍"等意思。
"书" originally meant "to dip the writing brush into the ink and write with it", and now it means "writing" or "book", etc.

[character evolution diagram: 聿(seal script) → 書(seal script) → 书]　shū
[stroke order grid: 丁 乙 书 书]

**2　汉字结构（2）：左右结构与左中右结构**
Structure of Chinese Characters (2): left-right and left-middle-right
