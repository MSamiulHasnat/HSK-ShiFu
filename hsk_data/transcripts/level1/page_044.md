### 2 在办公室 **In the office** 🎧 05-2

[photo: a young girl with curly hair and a headband, smiling outdoors on grass]

Nǐ nǚ'ér jǐ suì le?
A: 你女儿几岁了？

Tā jīnnián sì suì le.
B: 她今年四岁了。

**English Version**
A: How old is your daughter?
B: She is four years old.

**New Words**
4. 女儿 nǚ'ér n. daughter
5. 几 jǐ pron. how many
6. 岁 suì m. year (of age)
7. 了 le part. used at the end of or in the middle of a sentence to indicate a change or a new circumstance
8. 今年 jīnnián n. this year

### 3 在办公室 **In the office** 🎧 05-3

Lǐ lǎoshī duō dà le?
A: 李老师多大了？

Tā jīnnián wǔshí suì le.
B: 她今年 50 岁了。

Tā nǚ'ér ne?
A: 她女儿呢？

Tā nǚ'ér jīnnián èrshí suì.
B: 她女儿今年 20 岁。

[photo: an older woman with short hair and a younger woman with long hair, both in white, embracing and smiling]

**English Version**
A: How old is Professor Li?
B: She is 50 years old.
A: What about her daughter?
B: Her daughter is 20.

**New Words**
9. 多 duō adv. indicating degree or extent
10. 大 dà adj. (of age) old
