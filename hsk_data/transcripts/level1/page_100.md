## 汉字 Characters

**1** 认识独体字 Single-Component Characters

(1) "午"，表示一天中白天十一点到一点的一段时间。

"午" in Chinese refers to the period of time between 11 am and 1 pm.

[illustration: character evolution diagram 8-like twisted shape → 个-like form → 屮-like form → 午, labeled "wǔ"]

[stroke order grid showing: 丿 ケ ケ 午]

(2) "电"，本义是下雨时天上出现的锋利多齿的闪光，是一种物理现象，也是一种能源。

"电" originally referred to a sharp and multi-edged flash in the sky during a rain. It is both a physical phenomenon and a type of energy.

[illustration: character evolution diagram ⁇-like shape → 乙-like form → 甲-like form → 电, labeled "diàn"]

[stroke order grid showing: 丨 冂 田 日 电]
