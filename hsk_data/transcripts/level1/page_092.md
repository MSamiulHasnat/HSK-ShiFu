**3** 汉字偏旁 "囗" 和 "礻" Chinese Radicals: "囗" and "礻"

| 偏旁 Radical | 解释 Explanation | 例字 Example Characters |
|---|---|---|
| 囗 | 国字框，一般表示被困住、包围住。<br>The radical "囗" usually means being trapped or besieged. | 国 guó country, nation<br>困 kùn to be trapped |
