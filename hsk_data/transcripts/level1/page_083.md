**3** 汉字偏旁 "辶" 和 "门" Chinese Radicals: "辶" and "门"

| 偏旁 Radical | 解释 Explanation | 例字 Example Characters |
|---|---|---|
| 辶 | 走之旁，一般和走路有关系。<br>The radical "辶" is usually related to "walking". | 这 zhè this<br>送 sòng to send |
| 门 | 门字框，一般和房间、房门有关。<br>The radical "门" is usually related to a room or a door. | 问 wèn to ask<br>间 jiān *a measure word for rooms* |
