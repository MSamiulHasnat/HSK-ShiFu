### 2 汉语的轻声 The Neutral Tone

汉语中除了四声以外，还有一个读得又短又轻的声调，叫作"轻声"。
例如：

Apart from the four tones mentioned previously, there is another tone in Chinese, which is short and light, known as "the neutral tone". For example:

[photo: a young girl and a woman smiling, cheek to cheek] māma 妈妈
[photo: an older man and a boy reading a book together] yéye 爷爷
[photo: an older woman and a girl smiling together] nǎinai 奶奶
[photo: a man carrying a boy on his shoulders outdoors] bàba 爸爸
