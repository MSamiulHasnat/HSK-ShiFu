**3　汉字偏旁"钅"和"口"　Chinese Radicals: "钅" and "口"**

| 偏旁 Radical | 解释 Explanation | 例字 Example Characters |
|---|---|---|
| 钅 | 金字旁，一般和金属有关系。<br>The radical "钅" is usually related to metal. | 钟 zhōng clock<br>钱 qián money |
| 口 | 口字旁，一般和嘴巴有关系。<br>The radical "口" is usually related to the mouth. | 吃 chī to eat<br>喝 hē to drink |
