### 2 认识独体字 Single-Component Characters

（1）"口"，本义是嘴巴，字形像人张开的嘴巴。
The basic meaning of "口" is "mouth", and the character is shaped like a mouth.

[diagram: pictographic evolution of 口: a rounded open-mouth shape → a more angular shape → a square shape → 口]
kǒu
[handwriting practice grid showing character 口 traced in 4 strokes: 丨, 冂, 一 — stroke order boxes labeled: 丨 ㄇ 口]

（2）"见"，字形上边是"目"，下边是"人"，意思是"睁着眼睛看"。
Its traditional form of "见" has a "目 (eye)" on the top and a "人 (person)" at the bottom, meaning "watching with eyes open".

[diagram: pictographic evolution of 见: an eye-over-legs pictograph → oracle-bone style → seal script → 见]
jiàn
