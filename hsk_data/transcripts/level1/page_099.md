## 拼音 Pinyin

轻声的功能 Function of Neutral-Tone Syllables 🔊 11-4

汉语中轻声不仅是一种音变现象，而且还能区分词义。比如：东西，读作"dōngxī"指方向，读作"dōngxi"指物品。

The neutral tone in Chinese is not only a phonological phenomenon, but also a way to distinguish meanings. For example, the word "东西" refers to the directions east and west when read as "dōngxī", but when read as "dōngxi", it means "thing/stuff".
