**4　钱数的表达　Expression of the Amount of Money**

人民币的基本单位是"元"，口语中读作"块"。例如：

The basic unit of Renminbi (Chinese currency) is "元", usually replaced by "块" in spoken Chinese. For example:

[photo: a 1-yuan Chinese banknote reading "中国人民银行" and "1", with a portrait]
一元（块）
one yuan/kuai

[photo: a 5-yuan Chinese banknote reading "中国" and "伍元", with a portrait]
五元（块）
five yuan/kuai

[photo: a 10-yuan Chinese banknote reading "人民银行" and "拾圆", with a portrait]
十元（块）
ten yuan/kuai

[photo: a 50-yuan Chinese banknote reading "中国人民银行" and "50"/"伍拾元", with a portrait]
五十元（块）
fifty yuan/kuai

[photo: several 100-yuan Chinese banknotes stacked, showing "100" repeatedly, with a portrait]
一百元（块）
one hundred yuan/kuai
