### 课文
### Text

**1 在饭馆儿 In a restaurant** 🎧 08-1

Nǐ xiǎng hē shénme?
A: 你 想 喝 什么?

Wǒ xiǎng hē chá.
B: 我 想 喝 茶。

Nǐ xiǎng chī shénme?
A: 你 想 吃 什么?

Wǒ xiǎng chī mǐfàn.
B: 我 想 吃 米饭。

[photo: a black bowl of white rice with chopsticks, next to a cup of tea, on a bamboo placemat]

**English Version**
A: What would you like to drink?
B: I'd like some tea.
A: What would you like to eat?
B: I'd like rice.

**New Words**
1. 想　xiǎng　mod.　to want, would like [UNCLEAR: text cut off at page edge]
2. 喝　hē　v.　to drink
3. 茶　chá　n.　tea
4. 吃　chī　v.　to eat
5. 米饭　mǐfàn　n.　cooked rice
