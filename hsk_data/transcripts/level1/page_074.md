### 汉字
### Characters

**1　认识独体字　Single-Component Characters**

（1）"少"，意思是规模小，数量不多，与"多"相对。
In contrast to "多 (many)", "少" means "few/little", indicating a small scale or quantity.

[character evolution diagram: 小(with extra stroke) → 小 → 少(cursive) → 少]　shǎo
[stroke order grid: 丨 丨 小 少]

（2）"个"，本义是最小独立单位的人，指一个人。现在变为量词。
"个" originally meant "one individual person". Now it has become a measure word.

[character evolution diagram: 木(variant) → 小 → 亽 → 个]　gè
[stroke order grid: 人 人 个]

**2　汉字结构（3）：上下结构与上中下结构**
Structure of Chinese Characters (3): top-bottom and top-middle-bottom

合体结构还包括上下结构和上中下结构。上下结构的结构图形为［⊟］，上中下结构的图形为［目］。

Both the top-bottom structure and the top-middle-bottom structure are compound structures. The top-bottom structure is [⊟] and the top-middle-bottom structure is [目].
