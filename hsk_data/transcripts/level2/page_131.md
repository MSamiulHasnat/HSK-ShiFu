## 语音 Pronunciation

**感叹句的句调**　Intonation of an Exclamatory Sentence 🔊 14-5

汉语感叹句的句调一般为降调。例如：

A Chinese exclamatory sentence usually has a falling intonation. For example:

Jīntiān tiānqì zhēn hǎo a!
（1）今天天气真好啊！↘ [tone mark: falling arrow]

Zhège Hànzì zhēn nán xiě a!
（2）这个汉字真难写啊！↘ [tone mark: falling arrow]

Zhè jiàn yīfu tài piàoliang le!
（3）这件衣服太漂亮了！↘ [tone mark: falling arrow]

## 汉字 Characters

**汉字偏旁"雨"和"贝"**　Chinese Radicals: "雨" and "贝"

| 偏旁 Radical | 解释 Explanation | 例字 Example Characters |
|---|---|---|
| 雨 | 雨字头，一般与云、雨等天气现象有关系。<br>The radical "雨", appearing at the top of a character, is usually related to such weather phenomena as clouds and rain. | 雪　xuě　snow<br>雾　wù　fog, mist |
| 贝 | 贝字旁，一般与钱财、鼎类器物有关系。<br>The radical "贝" is usually related to money or utensils like an ancient cooking vessel. | 财　cái　wealth, money<br>货　huò　goods, commodity |
