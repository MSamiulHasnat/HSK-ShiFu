## 语音 Pronunciation

**用"吧"和"吗"构成的疑问句的句调** 🔊 15-5

Intonation of a Question Ending with "吧" or "吗"

用"吧"构成的疑问句的句调为降调，用"吗"构成的疑问句的句调为升调。例如：

A question ending with "吧" has a falling intonation, and one that ends with "吗" has a rising intonation. For example:

Zhè běn shū shì nǐ de ba?　　　　　　　Zhè běn shū shì nǐ de ma?
（1）这本书是你的吧？↘ [falling arrow]　　这本书是你的吗？↗ [rising arrow]

Míngtiān shì xīngqī èr ba?　　　　　　Míngtiān shì xīngqī èr ma?
（2）明天是星期二吧？↘ [falling arrow]　　明天是星期二吗？↗ [rising arrow]

Nǐ mǎi piào le ba?　　　　　　　　　Nǐ mǎi piào le ma?
（3）你买票了吧？↘ [falling arrow]　　　你买票了吗？↗ [rising arrow]

## 汉字 Characters

**汉字偏旁"山"和"大"**　Chinese Radicals: "山" and "大"

| 偏旁 Radical | 解释 Explanation | 例字 Example Characters |
|---|---|---|
| 山 | 山字旁，一般与山的名称、种类、形状及岛屿有关系。<br>The radical "山" is usually related to the various names, types and forms of mountains or to islands. | 岭　lǐng　ridge of a mountain<br>岖　qū　(used to describe mountain paths, etc.) rugged, rough |
| 大 | 大字旁，一般与人有关系。<br>The radical "大" is usually related to people. | 天　tiān　sky<br>夫　fū　husband |
