## 二、"教程"的特色

Èr, "Jiàochéng" de tèsè

English: II. Features of the "Course"

### 1. 以汉语水平考试大纲为依据，逐级编写"教程"

Yǐ Hànyǔ Shuǐpíng Kǎoshì dàgāng wéi yījù, zhú jí biānxiě "jiàochéng"

English: Writing the "Course" level by level based on the HSK Syllabus

汉语水平考试（HSK）共分六个等级，"教程"编教人员仔细研读了"大纲"和出题指南，并对大量真题进行了统计、分析。根据真题统计结果归纳出每册的重点、难点、语言点、话题、功能、场景等，在遵循HSK大纲词汇要求的前提下，系统设计了各级别的范围、课时等，具体安排如下：

Hànyǔ Shuǐpíng Kǎoshì (HSK) gòng fēn liù gè děngjí, "jiàochéng" biānjiào rényuán zǐxì yándúle "dàgāng" hé chūtí zhǐnán, bìng duì dàliàng zhēntí jìnxíngle tǒngjì, fēnxī. Gēnjù zhēntí tǒngjì jiéguǒ guīnà chū měi cè de zhòngdiǎn, nándiǎn, yǔyándiǎn, huàtí, gōngnéng, chǎngjǐng děng, zài zūnxún HSK dàgāng cíhuì yāoqiú de qiántí xià, xìtǒng shèjìle gè jíbié de fànwéi, kèshí děng, jùtǐ ānpái rúxià:

English: The HSK is divided into six levels in total. The "Course" editorial staff carefully studied the "Syllabus" and the question-writing guidelines, and conducted statistical analysis of a large number of real exam questions. Based on the statistical results of real questions, the key points, difficult points, language points, topics, functions, scenarios, etc. of each volume were summarized. Under the premise of following the HSK Syllabus vocabulary requirements, the scope, class hours, etc. of each level were systematically designed, with the specific arrangement as follows:

| 教材分册 (Volume) | 教学目标 (Teaching Goal) | 词汇量（词）(Vocabulary) | 教学时数（学时）(Teaching Hours) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 教程1 (Course 1) | HSK（一级）(HSK Level 1) | 150 | 30–34 |
| 教程2 (Course 2) | HSK（二级）(HSK Level 2) | 300 | 30–36 |
| 教程3 (Course 3) | HSK（三级）(HSK Level 3) | 600 | 35–40 |
| 教程4（上/下）(Course 4, Vol. 1/2) | HSK（四级）(HSK Level 4) | 1200 | 75–80 |
| 教程5（上/下）(Course 5, Vol. 1/2) | HSK（五级）(HSK Level 5) | 2500 | 170–180 |
| 教程6（上/下）(Course 6, Vol. 1/2) | HSK（六级）(HSK Level 6) | 5000 及以上 (5000 and above) | 170–180 |
| 总计：9册 (Total: 9 volumes) |  | 5000 以上 (5000 and above) | 510–550 |

这种设计遵循汉语国际教育的理念，注重教材的普适性、应用性和实用性，海内外教学机构可根据学时建议来设计每册书完成的年限。比如，一级的《教程1》规定用34学时完成，如果国内周课时是8课时的话，大概一个月左右就能学完；在海外如果一周是4课时的话，就需要两个月的时间能学完。以此类推。一般来说，学完《教程1》就能通过一级考试，同样学完《教程2》就能通过二级考试，等等。

Zhè zhǒng shèjì zūnxún Hànyǔ guójì jiàoyù de lǐniàn, zhùzhòng jiàocái de pǔshìxìng, yìngyòngxìng hé shíyòngxìng, hǎinèiwài jiàoxué jīgòu kě gēnjù xuéshí jiànyì lái shèjì měi cè shū wánchéng de niánxiàn. Bǐrú, yī jí de "Jiàochéng 1" guīdìng yòng 34 xuéshí wánchéng, rúguǒ guónèi zhōu kèshí shì 8 kèshí dehuà, dàgài yī gè yuè zuǒyòu jiù néng xuéwán; zài hǎiwài rúguǒ yī zhōu shì 4 kèshí dehuà, jiù xūyào liǎng gè yuè de shíjiān néng xuéwán. Yǐ cǐ lèituī. Yībān lái shuō, xuéwán "Jiàochéng 1" jiù néng tōngguò yī jí kǎoshì, tóngyàng xuéwán "Jiàochéng 2" jiù néng tōngguò èr jí kǎoshì, děngděng.

English: This design follows the philosophy of international Chinese language education, emphasizing the universality, applicability, and practicality of the teaching materials. Teaching institutions at home and abroad can design the timeline for completing each volume based on the recommended class hours. For example, Level 1's "Course 1" is designed to be completed in 34 class hours; if the domestic weekly class hours are 8 hours, it can generally be completed in about one month; if abroad it is 4 class hours per week, it would take two months to complete. And so on by analogy. Generally speaking, after completing "Course 1" one should be able to pass the Level 1 exam, and likewise after completing "Course 2" one should be able to pass the Level 2 exam, and so on.

### 2. 每册教材配有练习册，练习册中练习的形式与HSK题型吻合

Měi cè jiàocái pèiyǒu liànxí cè, liànxí cè zhōng liànxí de xíngshì yǔ HSK tí xíng wěnhé

English: Each volume of teaching material is accompanied by a workbook, and the format of the exercises in the workbook matches the HSK question types

为了使学习者适应HSK的考试题型，教材的各级练习册设计的练习题型均与HSK考试题型吻合，从练习的顺序到练习的结构等都与考题试卷保持一致，练习的内容以本课的内容为主，目的是学习者学完教材就能适应HSK考试，不需额外熟悉考试形式。

Wèile shǐ xuéxízhě shìyìng HSK de kǎoshì tíxíng, jiàocái de gè jí liànxí cè shèjì de liànxí tíxíng jūn yǔ HSK kǎoshì tíxíng wěnhé, cóng liànxí de shùnxù dào liànxí de jiégòu děng dōu yǔ kǎotí shìjuàn bǎochí yīzhì, liànxí de nèiróng yǐ běn kè de nèiróng wéi zhǔ, mùdì shì xuéxízhě xuéwán jiàocái jiù néng shìyìng HSK kǎoshì, bù xū éwài shúxī kǎoshì xíngshì.

English: In order for learners to adapt to the HSK exam question types, the exercise types designed for the workbooks at each level match the HSK exam question types; from the order of exercises to the structure of exercises, everything is kept consistent with the exam paper. The content of the exercises is mainly based on the content of this lesson, with the goal that learners will be able to adapt to the HSK exam right after finishing the textbook, without needing extra familiarization with the exam format.

### 3. 单独设置交际练习，紧密结合HSK口试内容

Dāndú shèzhì jiāojì liànxí, jǐnmì jiéhé HSK kǒushì nèiróng

English: Separately setting up communicative exercises, closely integrated with HSK oral exam content

在HSK考试中，口试独立于笔试之外，为了培养学生的口语表达能力，在教程中，每一课都提供交际练习，包括双人活动和小组活动等，为学习者参加口试提供保障。

Zài HSK kǎoshì zhōng, kǒushì dúlì yú bǐshì zhī wài, wèile péiyǎng xuéshēng de kǒuyǔ biǎodá nénglì, zài jiàochéng zhōng, měi yī kè dōu tígōng jiāojì liànxí, bāokuò shuāngrén huódòng hé xiǎozǔ huódòng děng, wèi xuéxízhě cānjiā kǒushì tígōng bǎozhàng.

English: In the HSK exam, the oral exam is independent of the written exam. In order to cultivate students' oral expression ability, every lesson in the course provides communicative exercises, including pair activities and group activities, etc., to provide a guarantee for learners taking the oral exam.
