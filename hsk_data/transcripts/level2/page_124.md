## 2　小组活动　Group Work

3~4人一组，用结构助词"着"描述图片内容。每组请一位同学做记录。

Work in groups of 3-4. Describe the picture using the structural particle "着". Each group chooses a member to take notes.

Yǒu liǎng ge xuésheng chuānzhe báisè de yīfu.
例如：有　两　个　学生　　穿着　白色的衣服。

[photo: five children sitting on a blue-and-grey checkered floor, listening to a woman (teacher) seated on a chair reading from a picture book]
