（2）含轻声音节的四音节词语 Quadrisyllabic Words with a Neutral Tone

含有轻声音节的四音节词语一般为形容词，第二个音节一般为轻声音节，第四个音节为重读音节。例如：
A quadrisyallbic word that has a neutral-tone syllable is usually an adjective, in which the second syllable is in the neutral tone and the fourth syllable is stressed. For example:

| duōduosuōsuō | huànghuangyōuyōu | mòmojījī | pīlipālā |
|---|---|---|---|
| 哆哆嗦嗦 • | 晃晃悠悠 • | 磨磨唧唧 • | 噼里啪啦 • |
