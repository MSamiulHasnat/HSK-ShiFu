## 汉字 Characters

### 汉字偏旁"扌"和"灬" Chinese Radicals: "扌" and "灬"

| 偏旁 Radical | 解释 Explanation | 例字 Example Characters |
|---|---|---|
| 扌 | 提土旁，多与泥土、土地、建筑物有关。 The radical "扌" is usually related to soil, land or buildings. | 块 kuài lump, piece (red text)<br>地 dì earth, land, ground (red text) |
| 灬 | 四点底，多与火及用火有关系。 The radical "灬" is usually related to fire or the use of fire. | 热 rè hot (red text)<br>黑 hēi black (red text) |

Note: the printed radical label in the first row appears as "扌" (tí tǔ páng); transcribed as shown in source heading "扌".
