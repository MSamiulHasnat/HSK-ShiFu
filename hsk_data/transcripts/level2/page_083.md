### 汉字偏旁"又"和"巾" Chinese Radicals: "又" and "巾"

| 偏旁 Radical | 解释 Explanation | 例字 Example Characters |
|---|---|---|
| 又 | 又字旁，字义比较多样。 The radical of "又" can have a variety of meanings. | 欢 huān merry, happy (red text)<br>对 duì right, correct (red text) |
| 巾 | 巾字底，大多与棉帛、纺织品有关系。 The radical "巾" is usually related to cotton or silk products or textiles. | 帮 bāng to help, to aid (red text)<br>帽 mào hat, cap (red text) |
