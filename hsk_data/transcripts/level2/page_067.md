### 2 汉字偏旁 "犭" 和 "心" Chinese Radicals: "犭" and "心"

| 偏旁 Radical | 解释 Explanation | 例字 Example Characters |
|---|---|---|
| 犭 | 反犬旁，一般和动物有关系。The radical "犭" is usually related to animals. | 猫　māo　cat (red text)<br>狗　gǒu　dog (red text) |
| 心 | 心字底，一般和人的思想活动及情感有关系。The radical "心" is usually related to one's mental activities and emotions. | 想　xiǎng　to think (red text)<br>念　niàn　to miss (red text) |
