俗语　见怪不怪　Jiàn guài bú guài
Common
Saying　One has become inured to the unusual

[Photo: a young woman with reddish hair in a blue top, shrugging with both hands raised, palms up]

"见怪不怪"，这里第一个"怪"是名词，意思是奇怪的
事物，第二个"怪"是动词，意思是感到奇怪。这句话的意
思是奇怪的事物看得多了，习惯了，也就不觉得奇怪了。

The first "怪" in the saying "见怪不怪" is a noun meaning
something strange, and the second "怪" is a verb meaning feeling
strange. The saying means that one has seen too many unusual
things to be surprised by any.
