## 汉字 Characters

### 1 汉字知识 Character Note

会意字 Associative Compounds

古代中国人有时用两个或两个以上的独体字组成一个汉字，这几个独体字的意思加起来就是整个汉字的意思。例如：

Gǔdài Zhōngguórén yǒushí yòng liǎng gè huò liǎng gè yǐshàng de dútǐzì zǔchéng yí gè Hànzì, zhè jǐ gè dútǐzì de yìsi jiā qǐlái jiùshì zhěnggè Hànzì de yìsi. Lìrú:

When creating Chinese characters, people in ancient times sometimes combined two or more single-component characters into one character. The meaning of the compound one was the meanings of the single-component characters added together. For example:

| 汉字 Character | 图片 Picture | 汉字的意思 Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 明 (míng) | [Illustration: an ancient oracle-bone/seal-script style glyph showing a swirl/circle shape resembling sun and moon combined] | 太阳和月亮在一起，光明。(Tàiyáng hé yuèliang zài yìqǐ, guāngmíng.) The sun and the moon are combined together to mean "bright." |
| 休 (xiū) | [Illustration: a drawing of a person leaning/sitting against a tree, next to the ancient script form 休] | 人靠在树上休息。(Rén kào zài shù shàng xiūxi.) A person is leaning against a tree. |
| 从 (cóng) | [Illustration: a drawing of one person walking behind another person, next to the ancient script form 从] | 一个人跟着另一个人。(Yí gè rén gēnzhe lìng yí gè rén.) A person is walking behind another. |
| 看 (kàn) | [Illustration: a drawing of a hand placed above an eye, next to the ancient script form 看] | 手放在眼睛上，看远方。(Shǒu fàng zài yǎnjing shàng, kàn yuǎnfāng.) Hold one's hand over one's eye to look into the distance. |

### 2 旧字新词 Word Game

读读下面的词语，想想组成的新词是什么意思。
Dúdú xiàmian de cíyǔ, xiǎngxiang zǔchéng de xīncí shì shénme yìsi.

Read the following words and guess the meanings of the newly-formed ones.

听 (tīng) —— 说 (shuō) → 听说 (tīngshuō)

有 (yǒu) —— 一点儿 (yìdiǎnr) → 有点儿 (yǒudiǎnr)

草 (cǎo) —— 地方 (dìfang) → 草地 (cǎodì)
